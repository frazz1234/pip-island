Meet Pip 🧡 a tiny buddy that lives in your Mac's notch.

When Claude Code (or Codex) needs your OK, Pip pops open right there. No more jumping back to the terminal. Allow, Always allow or Deny, and keep working ✨

Pip also:
🎵 shows your music, with AirPlay, plus your calendar and weather
🔔 brings your Mac notifications into the notch
🎯 opens a wheel of your apps, sites and commands with ⌃⌥Space
🗂️ converts, compresses or locks files when you drag + hold Shift
📱 lets you approve from your phone with Away mode

Updates itself automatically. macOS 14 or later.
